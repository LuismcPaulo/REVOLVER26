THE REASON FOR THIS SITE

This project was developed based on references from magazines of the 2000s, which popularized personality tests. As a child, I remember taking them, paying close attention to the results, truly believing they defined me. Strangely, these results led me to try to replicate these archetypes, until I understood how exhausting it is to continuously act on them. In a way, this simplification of women caused me, for a long time while I was forming myself as a woman, to avoid stating details about myself that contradicted these formulas. In this sense, this test aims to satirize this constant attempt to simplify women, which seeks to deconstruct them into numbers and codes, like machines. Stay resilient, and complex, xoxo.
