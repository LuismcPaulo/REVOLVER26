Stop the War

Across different regions, wars are forcing families to flee with almost no time to prepare. Many people are separated from loved ones and lose their homes, jobs, schools, and basic sense of safety.

When conflict destroys hospitals, roads, water systems, and electricity, daily life becomes a survival struggle. Children miss years of education, older people lose access to treatment, and communities face trauma that can last for generations.

Helping stop the war means protecting civilians, supporting humanitarian aid, speaking up for human rights, and backing peace efforts that push for ceasefire and long-term recovery. Every voice and every action can help save lives.
